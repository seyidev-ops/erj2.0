How Notion Turned 3 Hours of Daily Chaos Into 45 Minutes of Focused Work

The 4-page Notion workspace that Cohort 6 participants use to manage their job search and their remote work — including the exact page structure.

Oluwaseyi Ashiru 6 June 2026 5 min read

A To-Do List vs a System

A to-do list tells you what to do. A Notion workspace tells you why it matters, where you are, and what is blocking you. The difference is the difference between being busy and being productive.

The Cohort 6 Remote OS: 4 Pages

Page 1: Daily Dashboard

Three sections: Today's KPIs (what success looks like for this specific day), Work Blocks (two 90-minute sessions scheduled before the day begins), EOD Summary (completed by 5pm, no exceptions). This single page replaces 80% of unstructured to-do lists.

Page 2: Job Search Tracker

Company | Role | Applied Date | Stage | Contact | Follow-up Date | Notes. Every active application visible in one place. Nothing falls through a browser tab.

Page 3: Skills and Learning Log

Date | Resource | Key Learning | Applied How. Turns passive consumption into documented growth — directly useful for interview answers and CV achievement bullets.

Page 4: Weekly Review

Every Sunday: What moved forward? What stalled? What changes next week? Three questions, 20 minutes, one page. Non-negotiable for anyone serious about sustained progress.
