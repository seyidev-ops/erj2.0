Cohort 9 Begins Tonight: What Week One Looks Like

For everyone who enrolled, and everyone who did not. What happens in the next five days, and what to do if you missed the door.

Oluwaseyi Ashiru 3 August 2026 4 min read

Cohort 9 begins tonight, Monday 3 August, at 8:00 PM WAT. Enrolment closed yesterday evening.

If you are in

Your portal login arrives within 24 hours of your payment being confirmed. If it has not, send your receipt on WhatsApp and it will be sorted tonight.

Before the session: run the free CV self-scan and screenshot your score. Do not fix anything yet. That score is your before picture, and in Stage 4 you will want it.

Week one, Stage 1 — Remote Mindset Blueprint:

  • Day 1. Two protected deep-work blocks in your actual calendar, not in theory.
  • Day 2. The Pomodoro protocol, and finding where your day currently leaks.
  • Day 3. Three measurable KPIs each morning, one structured end-of-day report each evening.
  • Day 4. Full workspace audit — physical and digital, including the power and internet redundancy that interviewers ask about.
  • Day 5. Assembly. You leave with your personal remote operating system: calendar blocks, KPI sheet, tracker, EOD template.

Come with a notebook and something you actually want to get done this week. Stage 1 works best when applied to real work rather than an exercise.

If you missed it

Three honest options.

Wait for Cohort 10. Enrolment closes Sunday 30 August, 8:00 PM WAT, and the cohort begins Monday 31 August. Message us and you go on the list. Meanwhile everything free stays free: the CV self-scan, this blog, the live masterclass, and the open WhatsApp job channel. Someone who works through the free stack properly arrives at the next cohort ahead of where most people start.

Start with one stage. Stages can be taken individually — Stage 1 at ₦70,000 is the smallest honest entry point. It costs more overall than the complete programme, and it is a real start.

Consider whether you need training at all. If you are already employable and simply not being seen, the done-for-you placement engine is a different door, and the Inner Circle residency opens 31 August.
