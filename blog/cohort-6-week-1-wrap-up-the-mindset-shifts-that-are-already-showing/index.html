Cohort 6 Week 1 Wrap-Up: The Mindset Shifts That Are Already Showing Results

Six days in, and the breakthroughs are already happening. Here's what Cohort 6 participants are saying — and the three mindset shifts that are creating the fastest results.

Week 1: What Actually Happened

When we launched Cohort 6, we knew the curriculum was the strongest we'd ever built. What we didn't fully anticipate was how quickly participants would begin reporting real shifts.

By Day 3, the Cohort 6 WhatsApp group was filled with people sharing their ATS scan results. One participant went from a 31% CV match score to 84% in a single evening after applying the Stage 3 bullet rewrite formula. Another reported three LinkedIn recruiter messages within 48 hours of updating her headline.

The Three Mindset Shifts Showing the Fastest Results

1. From "I need experience" to "I need evidence"

The biggest barrier most participants brought in was the belief that they needed more certifications, more years, more something. What Stage 1 reframes is this: employers don't want experience for its own sake — they want evidence of what you can do. Your CV is an evidence document, not a biography.

2. From "I'll apply broadly" to "I'll apply precisely"

Mass applications to 40 generic roles produced zero callbacks. Targeted applications — 5 per week, each tailored — produced 3–4 callbacks from Week 2 onward for participants in previous cohorts who applied this approach.

3. From "remote work is for Westerners" to "remote work is for the prepared"

This is the shift that changes the trajectory. The barrier to global remote work from Africa is not geography — it is preparation. And preparation is exactly what this programme provides.
