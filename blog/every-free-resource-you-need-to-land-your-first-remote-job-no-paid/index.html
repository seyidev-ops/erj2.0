Every Free Resource You Need to Land Your First Remote Job (No Paid Course Required)

The paid course industry prefers you did not know about these. Every free tool and resource you need to go from zero to remote job offer — organised by stage.

Oluwaseyi Ashiru 10 June 2026 5 min read

Stage 1: Build the Foundation

  • Notion (free tier): Daily KPI dashboard, job tracker, EOD log. The free tier is more than sufficient for the entire job search system.
  • Cal.com (free): Professional scheduling link. More credible than "what time works for you" emails when booking calls.

Stage 2: Build Your Materials

  • Jobscan.co (free scans): Paste your CV against any job description. See your ATS match score before applying.
  • Canva (free tier): Clean, ATS-readable CV templates. Export as PDF.
  • Loom (free tier): Record a 2-minute portfolio walkthrough video. Embed in your Notion portfolio page.

Stage 3: Find and Apply

  • Remotive.io: Free. Filter by "Worldwide" for global remote roles with no geographic restriction.
  • Himalayas.app: Free. Every role is remote. Filter by location acceptance.
  • LinkedIn (free): Connection requests with notes do not require Premium. Your primary outreach channel is completely free.
  • Hunter.io (free tier): 25 email lookups per month. Enough to find email formats at target companies as a LinkedIn backup.

Stage 4: Get Paid

  • Wise (free account): Get a USD receiving account with no minimum balance. Most reliable for Nigerian remote workers.
  • Grey Finance (free account): Built for Nigerian remote workers receiving international payments. Excellent local support.

You do not need a paid course to land a remote job. You need the right information, in the right order, applied consistently. Everything listed above is free. The only cost is the discipline to use it.
