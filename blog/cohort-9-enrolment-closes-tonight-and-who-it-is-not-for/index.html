Cohort 9 Enrolment Closes Tonight — And Who It Is Not For

What the four stages actually contain, what each costs, and an honest list of the people who should not enrol today.

Oluwaseyi Ashiru 2 August 2026 5 min read

Enrolment for Cohort 9 closes tonight, Sunday 2 August, at 8:00 PM WAT. The cohort itself begins tomorrow evening, Monday 3 August, at 8:00 PM WAT.

Everything on this blog for the past month has been the free version of what we teach. This post is the paid version described plainly, including who should not buy it.

What the four stages are

Stage 1 — Remote Mindset Blueprint. Five days. Deep work blocks, daily KPIs, end-of-day reporting, and a full workspace audit. You leave with a working operating system for a day nobody supervises. ₦70,000

Stage 2 — The Digital Toolkit. Five days. Zoom, Meet and Teams properly; Asana, Trello or ClickUp; cloud recording and transcription; collaborative documents; and AI fluency layered across all of it. Ends in a capstone sprint you can show an employer. ₦130,000

Stage 3 — Async Communication Mastery. Five days. The zero-follow-up email framework, batch messaging, tone across text, and your own Working With Me manual. ₦70,000

Stage 4 — Start Your Remote Career. Five days. Remote-first CV built to clear the software, digital portfolio, optimised LinkedIn, and a recorded STAR interview video — reviewed as a set. ₦100,000

Taken separately that is ₦370,000. All four together is ₦250,000, so the complete programme saves ₦120,000. Every stage includes AI fluency, the workbooks and LMS access. The former paid job-feed benefit has since been retired; the current execution service is Done-For-You Application.

Who should not enrol tonight

If the money competes with rent or school fees. Do not borrow for this. Come to the next cohort. Everything free on this site stays free and it is genuinely useful — the CV scan, the blog, the masterclass, the open WhatsApp job channel.

If you want to be hired without applying. The training makes you hireable and gives you a system. It does not apply on your behalf. That is Stage 5, which is a separate, done-for-you thing.

If you cannot give it evening time for four weeks. Twenty training days with real deliverables. Someone who cannot attend will get a fraction of the value.

If you are already employable and well represented. If your CV clears the scan, your portfolio is live, and you are getting interviews but not offers, you do not need Stages 1 to 4. You need interview work, or simply better targets.

Who it is for

Someone who can do real work, cannot yet describe it in a way an international employer recognises, and would rather be walked through the twenty days than assemble them from blog posts over six months.

The pledge

Enrolment does not expire when the training does. You keep the community and the support until you are hired. That is the whole promise and it is the reason cohorts are capped.
